隐私政策

iSensor Data（传感器节点蓝牙配置工具）

本政策说明 iSensor Data（以下简称「本 App」）如何处理用户数据。本 App 由开发者个人/企业发布，用于通过低功耗蓝牙（BLE）配置 SDI-12 转 LoRaWAN 数据采集节点设备。

一、我们收集的信息

本 App 不收集任何个人信息，也不收集任何与设备、用户或位置相关的标识符。具体而言：

  • 不要求注册或登录账号，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、照片等个人数据；
  • 不收集设备标识符（如 IDFA、广告标识符），不包含任何第三方统计、广告或分析 SDK；
  • 不收集位置信息，蓝牙扫描仅用于查找并连接附近的采集节点硬件设备；
  • 不通过任何方式向服务器上传数据。

二、数据的存储与处理

所有设备参数、采集结果、通信日志均仅保存在您手机的本地存储中，用于在界面上展示与用户主动导出。卸载 App 会一并删除这些数据。除您主动复制、导出或分享外，数据不会离开您的设备。

三、网络通信

本 App 不访问互联网，不依赖任何后端服务。与设备之间的全部通信（指令下发、参数读写、日志）仅通过蓝牙在本地完成。App 不会在后台建立网络连接。

四、第三方服务

本 App 未集成任何会收集数据的第三方 SDK，不含第三方内容，不向任何第三方共享或出售数据。App 内仅打包了开源 UI 框架 uView UI（MIT 许可），该框架不收集任何数据、不发起网络请求。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 面向工程技术人员，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的任何信息。

六、政策更新

若本政策发生变更，更新后的内容将发布在本页面。如有重大变更，我们会在 App 内提示。

七、联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请发送邮件至：zhaotian7282@126.com。

Privacy Policy

iSensor Data (Bluetooth configuration tool for sensor nodes)

This policy explains how iSensor Data (the "App") handles user data. The App is used to configure SDI-12 to LoRaWAN sensor nodes over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information, and does not collect any device, user or location identifiers. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login is required; no name, phone number, email, contacts or photos are collected;
  • No device identifiers (such as IDFA or advertising identifiers) are collected; no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK is included;
  • No location data is collected. Bluetooth scanning is used only to find and connect to a nearby sensor node;
  • No data is uploaded to any server by any means.

2. Data storage and processing

All device parameters, sampling results and communication logs are stored only in local storage on your phone, for display in the App and for export initiated by you. Uninstalling the App deletes this data. Data does not leave your device unless you explicitly copy, export or share it.

3. Network communication

The App does not access the Internet and does not depend on any backend service. All communication with the device (commands, parameter read/write, logs) happens locally over Bluetooth. The App does not open network connections in the background.

4. Third-party services

The App includes no third-party SDK that collects data, contains no third-party content, and does not share or sell data to any third party. The only bundled library is the open-source UI framework uView UI (MIT license), which collects no data and makes no network requests.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for engineering and technical personnel, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. For material changes we will notify users inside the App.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please email: zhaotian7282@126.com.
