常见问题

  • 扫描不到设备：先按一下设备上的按键唤醒蓝牙，BLE 仅在按键 / 上电 / 复位后的 60 秒内开启，超时需重新唤醒。
  • 连接后无法读写参数：设备可能处于锁定状态，App 会自动尝试解锁；若失败，请在顶栏输入设备标签上的 6 位 AT PIN 后点「发送」。
  • 没有硬件也能体验：扫描页选择内置演示设备即可，界面、流程与真机完全一致。
  • 写入后提示未生效：App 会在写入后自动回读校验，请照提示确认当前值与期望值，或查看通信日志中的设备应答。

设备信息

  • 适配产品：SDI-12 转 LoRaWAN 数据采集节点（通用蓝牙模组）
  • 实机验证固件：US915 v1.3.2
  • 连接方式：低功耗蓝牙（BLE）串口，无需联网

FAQ

  • Device not found: press the button on the device to wake its BLE radio. BLE stays on for only 60 seconds after power-up, a button press or a reset; wake it again if it timed out.
  • Cannot read or write parameters after connecting: the device may be locked. The App tries to unlock it automatically. If that fails, type the 6-character AT PIN printed on the device label into the field in the top bar and tap Send.
  • No hardware available: on the scan screen, select the built-in demo device. The UI and flow are identical to a real device.
  • "Not applied" after saving: the App reads the value back after every write. Check the expected vs. actual value shown, or open the communication log to see the device response.

Device information

  • Supported product: SDI-12 to LoRaWAN data logger node (generic BLE module)
  • Verified firmware: US915 v1.3.2
  • Connection: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) serial, no Internet required
